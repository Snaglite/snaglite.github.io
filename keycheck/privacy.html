Privacy Policy — KeyCheck

Last updated: 8 November 2026

KeyCheck is an app for recording plot handovers: keys, meter readings, photos, signatures and project documents. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is kept, and your rights under the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data Controller

Jason O'Donoghue, independent developer of KeyCheck

Contact: Email

If you use iCloud sync, Apple may act as a separate data controller for processing under your Apple Account. Apple’s privacy policy: Apple Privacy Policy

Summary

  • Your projects are stored on your device. We (the developer) do not run any servers and never receive your project data.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync, your projects are stored in your own iCloud account, and in the iCloud account of anyone who shares a project with you.
  • Meter photos are read on your device. They are never sent anywhere to be read.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell data.

What Data the App Handles

1) Project content you enter

  • Projects, plots and addresses, key and fob records, meter readings and serial numbers, notes, diary entries, planning conditions and document tracking.
  • Photos you take or attach (for example meter photos), documents you import, and markup you add to them.
  • Names and signatures captured at handover, for example the person receiving keys and the people signing off a plot.
  • Branding, templates and app settings.

This content may include personal data about other people, such as purchasers, residents or colleagues. If you record it, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to do so and for telling those people how their information is used.

2) Location (optional)

  • If you allow location access, KeyCheck uses your current location while the app is open to centre the project map and show current weather.
  • To get the weather, your approximate coordinates are sent to Apple’s WeatherKit service. KeyCheck does not store your location or add it to your projects.
  • You can turn location access off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > KeyCheck. Only the map position and weather badge are affected.

3) Project addresses on the map

To show projects on the map, project addresses are sent to Apple Maps to find their position. No other project content is sent.

4) Meter recognition

Meter readings and serial numbers are read from photos using Apple’s on-device Vision and Apple Intelligence frameworks. This happens entirely on your device. Photos are not uploaded to read them.

5) Diagnostics

  • KeyCheck does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Legal Bases for Processing

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)): to provide the app’s features.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): for optional features you turn on, such as iCloud sync, sharing and location access.

iCloud Sync and Sharing

  • iCloud sync is off until you turn it on in Settings. When on, your projects, plots, meter readings, photos, signatures, documents, branding and templates are stored in your iCloud account so they appear on your other devices.
  • If you share a project, the people you invite can see it, and edit it if you allow that. Shared projects are stored in the iCloud account of the person who shared them.
  • You can stop sharing or turn off sync at any time.

Exports

When you export a PDF report or a KeyCheck project file, it is created on your device. Where it goes next (email, Files, AirDrop and so on) is your choice, and that service’s privacy terms apply.

Subscriptions and Billing

KeyCheck offers auto-renewing subscriptions with a free trial for eligible new subscribers. Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active to unlock editing. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app. If iCloud sync is on, synced data stays in iCloud until you delete it in the app, or remove KeyCheck’s data from iCloud in your device’s iCloud settings. If the app ever finds data it cannot read, it keeps a copy in a recovery folder on your device so nothing is lost. You can share or remove that copy from Settings.

Your Rights

  • Right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off iCloud sync or location access.
  • Right to complain to a supervisory authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Because your data stays on your device and in your iCloud, you can view, export and delete it directly in the app. For any other request, contact us at Email.

Children’s Privacy

KeyCheck is a business app and is not directed at children.

Security

Data is kept using Apple’s storage frameworks, and iCloud data is protected by Apple’s security. Keep your device and Apple Account secure, for example with a passcode and two-factor authentication.

Third Parties

  • Apple Inc. provides iCloud, payments, Apple Maps and Apple Weather. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • KeyCheck does not include any third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services.
