KeyCheck Beta Tester Guide

Thank you for testing KeyCheck. This guide covers the main things to try and how to tell us what you find.

1Getting started

Install the beta, unlock editing and find your way around.

Installing

  1. Open the invitation email or link on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Install Apple's free TestFlight app if you're asked to.
  3. In TestFlight, tap Install next to KeyCheck.

When we release a new beta, TestFlight lets you know. Updating keeps your projects.

Unlocking editing (free during testing)

KeyCheck needs a KeyCheck Pro subscription to create projects or make changes. Without one, you can still open and export everything, and a View only notice appears at the top of each project.

  1. Tap View plans on the View only notice, or try to create a project.
  2. Choose a plan and start the free trial.
You won't be charged

Purchases in TestFlight are test purchases, and Apple doesn't take any money for them. Apple speeds up trials and renewals in TestFlight, so your subscription may seem to end after a day or so. If it does, subscribe again the same way.

The sample projects

The first time you open KeyCheck, it adds two example sites to explore:

  • Meadow View Phase 2: a housing site with plots at different stages, from not started to handed over and signed.
  • Riverside Business Park: a commercial site, using units instead of plots.

Samples have a Sample badge. They stay on your device and are never synced or shared, so you can change or break anything in them. Delete them when you've finished. To get them back, go to Settings → Add sample projects. Settings is on the Projects screen.

Try this first

Open Meadow View Phase 2 and look around. Compare a handed-over plot (plots 1 to 4) with one that hasn't been started (plots 12 to 15).

Setting yourself up

In Settings:

  • You → Your name: this appears as "Prepared by" on your PDFs. On shared projects, it also shows who made each change.
  • Branding: add your company's logo, name and colours for your PDFs. Tap Preview sample PDF to see how they look.

Creating your own project

  1. On the Projects screen, tap New project.
  2. Enter the site name and address. You can add plot numbers straight away, for example 1-20, 24, 30-35, or add them later.
  3. Tap Create.

To change what's on the handover form, open the project and tap Project setup (the sliders button, top right). From there:

  • Form items changes the list of keys, meters, certificates and sign-off roles.
  • Addresses and meter details… imports plot addresses and meter details from a spreadsheet saved as CSV.

2Recording a plot handover

Open a project and tap a plot. The handover form has these sections, top to bottom.

SectionWhat you record
DetailsPostal address, tenure (for example Rent or Shared Ownership) and handover date.
KeysHow many of each key are handed over. Fill with usual quantities fills in the usual numbers for you.
MetersElectricity, gas and water readings, serial numbers, suppliers and photos. See Meter scanning.
Instructions & certificatesThe documents for the plot, such as the gas safety certificate and EPC.
Defects / outstanding itemsSnags still to fix, with due dates.
Sign-offSignatures from the client and contractor.
NotesAnything else.

The bar near the top shows how complete the form is. Tap it to see What's left and jump straight to it. Use Previous and Next to move between plots without going back to the list.

Certificates and documents

Each document has two buttons:

  • ✓ (green): you have it.
  • ✗ (red): you've asked for it and are still waiting.

Leave both unselected if nobody has checked it yet. If a document doesn't apply to this plot, touch and hold it and choose Not applicable.

KeyCheck saves the date each time you change a document. A grey line under the document's name shows where it stands, such as "Waiting 5 days · chased yesterday". Tap the document's name to see its full history or correct a date.

Tip

Chasing the same documents for lots of plots? Tap Project setup → Chase or update documents… to update them all at once.

Snags

Tap Add outstanding item to add a snag. When it's fixed, tap the circle beside it. KeyCheck records today as the completed date, and you can tap the date to change it.

Signatures

In Sign-off, type each person's name, tap Sign and have them sign in the box, then tap Done. To redo a signature, tap Clear and sign again. If the plot doesn't have a handover date yet, the first signature sets it to today.

Saving time on similar plots

Plots on the same street are often set up the same way. Use Copy from to copy keys, suppliers and documents from the previous plot. Or open Project setup → Apply setup to several… to copy one plot's setup to lots of plots in one go.

Project-wide records

These are on the project screen, above the plot list:

  • Project documents: site-wide documents, such as the fire strategy or warranties.
  • Communal keys: keys and fobs for bin stores, cycle stores, plant rooms and so on.
  • Project log: dated notes for site visits, issues, decisions and chases.

3Meter scanning

KeyCheck can read meter readings and serial numbers from a photo.

Experimental

Meter scanning will sometimes misread a number, so always check what it found against the photo.

Reading a meter

  1. Open a plot and scroll to a meter, for example Electricity.
  2. Tap Photo to take a picture, or Library to choose one you've already taken.
  3. KeyCheck reads the photo and shows what it Found on photo.
  4. Check each value. If a box already has a value, turn on that row to replace it.
  5. Tap Add to Form.

If KeyCheck can't find anything, tap Done and type the values in yourself. The photo is still saved to the meter.

KeyCheck may warn you that:

  • the photo looks like a different plot, if it spots another plot number or a serial number from another plot
  • it looks like a different type of meter, for example a gas meter photo added to Electricity

To read a saved photo again, tap the photo, then tap Read meter.

Tips for good results

  • Get close and fill most of the photo with the display and serial number.
  • Avoid glare from the flash and shadows across the display.
  • Plot marks written on the meter box, such as "PLOT 30", help KeyCheck check you're on the right plot.

Privacy

Photos are read on your device only. They're never sent anywhere to be read. To stop KeyCheck reading photos automatically, turn off Settings → Meter photos → Auto-read meter photos. On recent iPhones with Apple Intelligence on, readings may be more accurate. Settings shows whether it's available.

What to tell us

Which meter types it reads well or badly. Please send the photo as well, if you're happy to.

4PDF reports

All exports are in the project's Share and export button (top right).

ExportWhat it is
Export this handover
(on a plot)		The handover form for one plot, with keys, meters, documents, snags and signatures.
Export all handover formsEvery plot's handover form.
Export progress report…A site summary for meetings or the client: what's changed, documents not received with how long you've waited, the project log and plot notes.
Share editable projectA .keycheck file a colleague can open in KeyCheck and edit. It's a separate copy, not kept in sync with yours.

Before the progress report is made, you choose:

  • Period: how far back "What's changed" looks.
  • Include: which sections to show.
  • Count as the latest report: turn this on to start the next "Since last report" period from this one.

Communal keys and project documents also have their own PDFs, on their screens.

When the PDF is ready, the share sheet opens. You can email it, save it to Files, AirDrop it or print it.

What to tell us

Anything cut off, overlapping or hard to read, and anything you'd expect to see on the report that's missing.

5iCloud sync and sharing

Keep your devices up to date, and work on a site with your team.

Syncing your own devices

iCloud sync is off until you turn it on.

  1. Go to Settings → iCloud sync → Turn on iCloud sync… and confirm.
  2. Do the same on your other iPhone or iPad, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Your projects, photos and signatures are stored in your own iCloud. KeyCheck never sends them anywhere else. The Status line shows when it last synced. Tap Sync now to sync straight away. Sample projects never sync.

Sharing a project with your team

  1. Open a project (not a sample) and tap Share and export → Share with others….
  2. Choose how to send the invitation, for example Messages or Mail, and whether people can make changes or view only.
  3. When they tap the link, the project opens in KeyCheck on their device. If their iCloud sync is off, KeyCheck asks them to turn it on.

Everyone sees each other's changes when their devices are online. A badge shows a project is shared. To add or remove people later, use Share and export → Manage sharing….

Your teammates need to be testers too, with KeyCheck installed from TestFlight.

What to try

  • Edit the same plot on two devices. Do both sets of changes end up on both devices?
  • Make changes with no signal, then reconnect. Do they sync?
  • Share a project as view only. Is the other person unable to change it?
What to tell us

Anything that doesn't appear on the other device, appears twice or goes missing. Tell us roughly when it happened and which devices you used.

6Sending feedback

Your feedback goes straight to the developer through TestFlight.

From inside KeyCheck (best for problems on screen)

  1. Take a screenshot (side button and volume up together).
  2. Tap the screenshot preview in the corner.
  3. Tap Done or the share button, then choose Share Beta Feedback. You can draw on the screenshot to point things out.
  4. Add a note and tap Submit.

From the TestFlight app

Open TestFlight, tap KeyCheck, then tap Send Beta Feedback.

If KeyCheck crashes

TestFlight may ask whether to share details of the crash. Please tap Share and add a line about what you were doing when it happened.

What helps most

  • What you did: the taps, in order. For example: "Opened plot 7, tapped Photo on Gas, took a photo".
  • What you expected, and what actually happened.
  • Your device, for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air.
  • A screenshot or screen recording.

Ideas are welcome too. If there's something you do on site that KeyCheck doesn't help with yet, let us know.

A note on your data

When you send feedback, you choose what to include. Please don't send screenshots showing residents' personal details. Use the sample projects to show a problem if you can.
